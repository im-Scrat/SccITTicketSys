## 17. Known limitations

1. **The documentation now asserts a trigger count that will change.** When
   WP-2.4b lands, "nine of twelve" becomes eleven of twelve; when WP-2.7c lands,
   twelve. The per-trigger table makes that a one-row edit rather than a hunt,
   but it is a number that must be maintained.

2. **Superseded plans are kept, not deleted.** The WP-2.6b sequencing paragraph
   remains with a superseded-by-delivery block beneath it. That preserves the
   decision history at the cost of a reader having to read both. Deleting it
   would have been tidier and less honest.

3. **DD-63 and DD-64 document work that does not exist.** The digest decision and
   the rollback model are recorded in full — including hazards — before either
   is built or exercised. They are decisions, not descriptions, and are labelled
   as such.

4. **`.docx` deliverables were not regenerated.** `deliverables/word/` still
   carries the pre-reconciliation build. Regenerating is a separate authorized
   step through `docs/build_*.py`; this checkpoint touched only the canonical
   Markdown, per the strict scope rule.

5. **Line endings.** The three files are CRLF in the working tree and git
   normalizes them on commit, as it did before this change. The diff is 221/20
   lines, confirming no mass rewrite occurred.
