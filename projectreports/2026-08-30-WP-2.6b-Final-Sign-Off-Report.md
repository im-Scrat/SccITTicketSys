## 11. Cleanup performed

Executed as a **single transaction** with pre-commit assertions that would have
aborted the whole operation had genuine data been touched. A verified backup was
taken first: `backups/sccit_prod-preCLEANUP-20260830-213102.sql.gz` — gzip OK,
82 `CREATE TABLE`s, ZZ-VERIFY data present, complete trailer.

| Records deleted | Count |
|---|---|
| `activity_logs` (verification users only) | 27 |
| `login_history` (verification users only) | 21 |
| `qr_scan_logs` | 7 |
| `users` (the five approved accounts) | 5 |
| `tickets` (`TKT-2026-00001/2/3`) | 3 |
| `qr_codes` | 3 |
| `work_support_requests` · `…_items` | 2 · 2 |
| `pc_units` | 2 |
| `password_reset_tokens` | 2 |
| `maintenance_records` · `repair_images` · `technician_assignments` | 1 · 1 · 1 |
| `pc_specifications` · `assets` · `hardware_models` · `hardware_components` | 1 each |
| `buildings` (cascading its floor and room) | 1 |
| **Temporary plaintext credential artifact** | overwritten, then unlinked |

Deletion order followed the actual foreign-key graph, read from
`pg_constraint` rather than assumed. `work_support_requests` (RESTRICT on both
`pc_units` and `users`) and `maintenance_records`/`technician_assignments`
(RESTRICT on `users`) were removed before their parents.

**Audit and login history were deleted by actor, not wholesale**, because
`activity_logs.user_id` and `login_history.user_id` are `SET NULL`: deleting the
users first would have orphaned those rows and made them indistinguishable from
the genuine administrator's own history.
