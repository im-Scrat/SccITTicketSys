## 13. Remaining deployment-time actions

For whoever runs the first release — these are the actions that close F-2 and
F-3, and they can only be done by deploying:

1. **Deploy through `sh scripts/deploy-prod.sh`**, not by hand. The manual path
   is what produced the incompatible `rollback-preWP26b` images.
2. **Confirm the release was recorded** — `releases/<sha>/manifest.json` exists
   and `sh scripts/releases.sh list` shows the release with both images present.
3. **Confirm the immutable tags exist** — `sccit/app:prod-<sha>` and
   `sccit/web:prod-<sha>`.
4. **Confirm the pre-deploy snapshot** was taken and is named in the manifest.
5. **Re-run the production smoke** — `sh scripts/smoke.sh --stack prod` and
   `sh scripts/e2e.sh --project smoke`; expect 13/13 and 5/5.
6. **Verify F-1 on the production target** once deployed: a scanned label,
   signed in, must land on `/app/qr/{code}`.
7. **After the second deployment**, verify a real rollback against a retained
   release in a controlled window. Only then are C and D fully PASS.

Until step 2 succeeds, keep the `rollback-preWP26b` images: they are the only
rollback path that currently exists, even though the new tooling cannot use them.
